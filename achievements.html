CELEBRATING MILESTONES

Achievements & Dreams

From fragile thatch roots to triumphant graduations — witness how patience, science, and boundless love create daily miracles.

PERMANENT SANCTUARY

From Thatched Roof to Concrete Haven

Amidst the various difficulties that we faced, we tried our best to provide balanced diet, clean water and healthy hygienic atmosphere to our differently abled and mentally challenged children. We did succeed in putting up a solid concrete building for our children and moving into it from the old thatched roof.

01

Unique Integrated Living

Prema Vasam is the only home that takes care of a large number of special children along with normal children. This integration develops sensitivity among normal children to attend to the needs of the mentally challenged and differently abled.

02

Bedridden Children Now Walking

Constant individual care, regular physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and special education have enabled a good number of mentally challenged children to talk and walk independently.

03

Mothers Living & Serving

The mothers of a number of differently abled and mentally challenged children live along with their children in our home and render their whole-hearted service to all the inmates with tender loving care.

04

Indra's Educational Triumph

Indra, the first child affected by Polio with scoliosis, completed SSLC with 84%, graduated from St. Joseph's College, earned an MCA from Anna University in first class, and completed Special Education to lead Prem Illam.

05

Manjampadi Boys Home

A Boys home established at Manjampadi in Arakonam houses 30 adolescent boys post-8th standard, granting them continuous secondary coaching and vocational readiness.

06

Expert Clinical Care Team

A team of good qualified physiotherapists, occupational therapists, and teachers trained in special education cater to the needs of our children, rejoicing at each child's progress.

THE ROAD AHEAD

Our Cherished Dreams

We are actively striving to expand our sanctuary with three critical goals:

👶

Child Adoption Center

Establishment of a specialized Adoption Center to shelter, protect, and give precious life to abandoned neonatal babies.

🏫

Dedicated Inclusive School

Building our own full-fledged school building for our children and underprivileged neighborhood kids with customized sensory amenities.

🌱

Self-Sustaining Programs

Developing cottage industries, organic farming, and skill-workshops to attain financial self-sufficiency for the future.
