REHABILITATION & HEALING

Our Daily Activities & Therapies

A comprehensive holistic approach blending clinical therapy, special education, expressive arts, and sports to nourish body, mind, and spirit.

01
DAILY • 9:30 AM TO 3:30 PM

Physiotherapy • Clinical Rehabilitation

🦽

A team of physiotherapists headed by Mr. Robinson gives physiotherapy to our children affected by cerebral palsy, autistic, and mentally challenged children every day from 9:30 AM to 3:30 PM.

They provide therapeutic exercises such as electrical stimulation, muscular stimulation, TENS to remove pain, contracture removing, stretching exercises, strengthening exercises and so on. We have electrical stimulator machines called Electro Care, parallel bars, static cycles, physioballs, and walkers.

Mr. Robinson & Physio Team
Remarkable Milestones:

“Regular physiotherapy results in remarkable improvement and development among our children. To cite a few examples: Rohit, a bedridden child suffering from cerebral palsy, is able to walk after two years of physiotherapy! Some of our physically challenged children — Meenakshi, Senthil Kumari, John, and Rithvik — are able to walk without support.”

02
SENSORY & TONE NORMALIZATION

Occupational Therapy

🧩

Sometimes our children do stay idle, and some of the children are bedridden — there is a compulsory need for occupational therapy.

We have fur toys, musical toys, standing frames, CP chairs, and building blocks to enhance occupational therapy for our children. In occupational therapy we give exercises to normalize tone, stretching, and positioning; to relieve tightness, maintained stretch is given. Milestones are achieved through different positioning and providing sensations of objects for grasping.

Of course, occupational therapy plays an important role in the lives of our special children. It makes them active; it gives a satisfying feeling to our special children because they could do something.

Sensory Stimulation & Toys
03
IQ-ADAPTED CURRICULUM

Special Education & Activities of Daily Living (ADL)

📚

Special children do have learning disabilities and are slow learners. Hence based on their IQ testing, they are categorized and provided with colorful toys, coloring books, clay, puzzles, matching cards, simple vocabulary, color concepts, shapes, simple arithmetic, and simplified academic skills.

Whereas for the trainable group, we teach ADL (Activities of Daily Living) to reach our main goal — to make them “independent”. We play music and songs as part of special education. Some of our children have made significant improvements: writing their names, reciting rhymes, naming objects, and excelling in self-care.

Special Education Class
04
SPORTS, DANCE & SCHOOL COACHING

Play Therapy, Kalakshetra Dance & School Coaching

🎭

Integration of normal children with special children in life activities plays an important and therapeutic role in the lives of our special children. Though our normal children are enrolled in our neighborhood schools, they also receive coaching and educational counseling.

Play Therapy & Games
