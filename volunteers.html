Words from Our Global Family

★★★★★

“I was a volunteer for 5 months at Prema Vasam. I left in January 2015 but I still think about them nearly every single day. I had to tell a lot of people, especially my family, about my experiences. Why...? Because I got to know and feel there that everyone has the right to be appreciated and to get love independent of origin, religion, or a disability. Prema Vasam is not just an orphanage — it is a big family of more than 200 people, where everyone can find a home.”

🇦🇹
Pia Zefferer Volunteer from Austria (5 months)
★★★★★

“The motto of Prema Vasam is ‘to serve... not to be served...’ but my time here has shown that you can be served by those who you are also serving. The love that I have felt from my Prema Vasam family has enriched my life more than words can describe. I am greatly blessed to have spent time in this beautiful place.”
