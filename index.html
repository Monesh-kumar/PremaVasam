OUR FOUNDATION & HISTORY

The Soul and Stories of Prema Vasam

Click through the chapters below to discover our beginnings, key champions, and miraculous milestones.

CHAPTER 1 • OUR PURPOSE

A Haven of Love for 200+ Special & Less Fortunate Children

PREMA VASAM is a registered non-profit charitable residential home (Reg. No. 419 of 1999) caring for mentally & physically challenged and underprivileged children.

It is the only home in Tamil Nadu uniquely integrating special-needs children (over 132 individuals with cerebral palsy, autism, intellectual disabilities, and multiple challenges) alongside school-going underprivileged children. This seamless integration develops sensitivity and empathy among normal children while offering therapeutic companionship.

Meaning of Prema Vasam: "A place of divine love or where eternal love resides."
CHAPTER 2 • THE NAME THAT STARTED IT ALL

Precious Gift — Prem

On May 14th, 1999, an abandoned baby boy was discovered by founder Selvyn Roy in a dying stage near Aminjikarai, Chennai.

Though severely mentally and physically challenged, bed-ridden, and completely dependent, this little boy radiate an infectious, angelic smile. Selvyn named him Prem.

"Later, the place where Prem lives and smiles became PREMA VASAM — our entire sanctuary was founded in honor of Prem."
CHAPTER 3 • INSPIRATION OF EMPOWERMENT

The Inception & Indra's Miraculous Journey

Prema Vasam was officially inaugurated on June 2nd, 1999 when Mr. Selvyn Roy decided to educate Indra, a 14-year-old girl suffering from severe Polio and scoliosis.

Indra had been in deep despair due to her inability to attend school. Under Prema Vasam's daily care and coaching, Indra joined in the 8th standard, scored an incredible 420/500 marks (84%) in her SSLC Board exams, and earned her undergraduate degree at St. Joseph's College.

She completed her Master of Computer Applications (MCA) First Class from the prestigious Anna University and completed Special Education. Today, Indra proudly serves as the Director of Prem Illam in Maduranthakam!

CHAPTER 4 • VISIONARY LEADERSHIP

Founder & Administrator — Mr. Selvyn Roy

Mr. Selvyn Roy is a postgraduate Clinical Psychologist with diplomas in psychological counseling and guidance.

Before establishing Prema Vasam, he dedicated his career at MITHRA (Rehabilitation of Mentally & Physically Challenged Children), served at the Clarke School for the Deaf and Dumb, and volunteered at the renowned Prithipura Infant Home in Sri Lanka.

For 25+ continuous years, he has guided Prema Vasam with an open-door policy — never turning down any child in need, irrespective of caste, religion, or severity of disability.

CHAPTER 5 • THE HEART OF OPERATIONS

Coordinator — Miss Mumtaj

Miss Mumtaj is a certified Multi-Rehabilitational Worker who has worked tirelessly and selflessly at Prema Vasam since the year 2000.

She oversees all clinical rehabilitation, medical interventions, accounts, volunteer orientations, and daily food provisions. When any child falls sick, she instructs nurses with intimate knowledge of each special child's medical condition.
