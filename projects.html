UNIT OF PREM CHARITABLE TRUST

Prem Niketan

Prem Niketan was founded in 2011 as an essential extension of our social service, addressing the urgent demand for specialized long-term rehabilitation and skill development.

Prem Niketan provides vocational training, tailored therapies, and peaceful community living for growing young adults who require continual encouragement and self-reliance guidance.

📍 Chennai • Tamil Nadu 🌐 www.premniketan.org

ESTABLISHED 2017
🌸
HOME FOR GIRL CHILDREN

Prem Illam • Maduranthakam

With the number of children increasing every year, Prem Illam was founded in 2017 at Sirunallur Village in Maduranthakam as a loving, secure sanctuary specially designated for girl children.

Prem Illam is directed with love by Indra — the very first girl child taken in by Selvyn Roy in 1999! Having earned her MCA and Special Education qualifications, Indra now serves as mother, director, and protector for scores of young girls, proving the motto: “To serve, not to be served.”

📍 Sirunallur Village, Maduranthakam 🌐 www.premillam.org

ARAKONAM BOYS HOME

Manjampadi Boys Home

Established at Manjampadi in Arakonam to ensure teenage boys receive dedicated secondary and higher schooling coaching, sports training, and disciplined moral guidance.

After completing the 8th standard at Prema Vasam, the older boys transition to this home where currently over 30 young boys reside, pursue their dreams, and support each other like brothers.

📍 Manjampadi, Arakonam, Tamil Nadu 👥 30+ Young Boys Under Active Mentorship
